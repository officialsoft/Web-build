about

Still early. Documenting it anyway.

I'm learning two things at once that turn out to reinforce each other: building web applications, and testing the networks and systems they run on. Understanding how things break makes me build them more carefully. Understanding how things are built makes me better at finding where they break.

I don't have a long portfolio yet — most of what's here is coursework, home-lab exercises, and small builds. What I do have is a consistent log of what I've studied, practiced, and shipped, updated as I go. Consider this page a work-in-progress by design.
